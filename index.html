About Me

Infrastructure that holds —
at scale, under pressure.

Solutions Architect · digi edZe Soft Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

I'm a Solutions Architect at digi edZe Soft Solutions, a multi-cloud digital transformation company with delivery presence across India, UAE, Oman, KSA, and the USA. My work sits at the intersection of pre-sales architecture, RFP strategy, and hands-on technical delivery for complex enterprise engagements.

My foundation is 15+ years of Linux and SRE practice — from bare-metal system engineering to modern Kubernetes-native architectures. I've designed OCI Dedicated Region (DRCC) environments, Oracle FSDR disaster recovery topologies (RPO <5 min / RTO <4 hrs), and end-to-end observability stacks for demanding enterprise clients.

Currently deepening expertise in AIOps and GenAI for cloud operations — building log anomaly detectors, LLM-powered alert triage bots, and OCI audit log analysers that cut through noise and deliver actionable signal to SRE teams.

Outside of architecture work, I write about what I've experienced — migration pitfalls, DR design patterns, and the practical path from infrastructure engineer to applied AI practitioner.
